# Style Templates

Use these templates when you need a stronger style steer after the user selects a scene.

## Work Chat

### Default

Natural, friendly, efficient, low-friction.

### Variants

- `Friendly push`: Move the task forward without sounding like a demand.
- `Soft reminder`: Nudge gently when waiting on something.
- `Calm clarification`: Clear up confusion without blame.

## Manager Communication

### Default

Respectful, steady, concise, reliable.

### Variants

- `Status update`: Lead with progress, then blockers, then next steps.
- `Request support`: State what is needed, why it matters, and what decision is requested.
- `Explanation and recovery`: Acknowledge the issue, explain briefly, then focus on remedy.

## Work Email

### Default

Professional, structured, polite, lean.

### Variants

- `Formal concise`: Tight and businesslike.
- `Warm professional`: Slightly more human while staying clear.
- `Follow-up`: Easy to scan and action-oriented.

## Networking

### Default

Warm, sincere, positive, low-pressure.

### Variants

- `First outreach`: Brief intro plus a specific reason for reaching out.
- `Follow-up note`: Gentle reconnection without guilt or pressure.
- `Thank-you`: Appreciative, concrete, and not overdone.

## Version Strategy

When generating multiple versions, vary along one or two axes only:

- softer vs firmer
- more formal vs more natural
- more concise vs slightly warmer

Do not vary every dimension at once. The user should immediately understand why version A differs from version B.
