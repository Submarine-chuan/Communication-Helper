# Good Examples

Store reusable positive examples here. Keep them anonymous and pattern-based.

## Entry Format

Use this compact format for new entries:

```text
Scene:
Intent:
Expression:
Why it works:
```

## Seed Examples

### Example 1

Scene: Work Chat
Intent: Follow up on a needed file without sounding pushy.
Expression: 方便的话，想和你确认一下那份材料今天是否能发我，我这边拿到后就可以继续往下接了。
Why it works: It keeps the ask clear, explains the dependency, and avoids sounding accusatory.

### Example 2

Scene: Manager Communication
Intent: Report delay while keeping accountability.
Expression: 这个事项目前比原计划慢了一点，主要卡在数据确认上。我今天会先把可推进的部分收完，明天中午前再向您同步最新进展。
Why it works: It acknowledges the issue, gives a reason without making excuses, and immediately provides a recovery plan.

### Example 3

Scene: Networking
Intent: Request a short chat after a prior introduction.
Expression: 很高兴之前有机会认识您。最近我在关注您提到的这个方向，如果您方便的话，我想找个合适时间向您请教二十分钟。
Why it works: It is respectful, specific, and low-pressure.
