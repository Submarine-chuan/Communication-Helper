# Bad Examples

Store examples here when the user says something feels fake, heavy, or too AI-like.

## Entry Format

Use this compact format for new entries:

```text
Scene:
Intent:
Expression:
Why it fails:
```

## Seed Examples

### Example 1

Scene: Work Chat
Intent: Remind a colleague to send a file.
Expression: 辛苦您在百忙之中尽快将相关材料发送给我，以便我能够顺利推进后续工作，万分感谢您的理解与支持。
Why it fails: It sounds inflated, stiff, and far heavier than a normal work chat.

### Example 2

Scene: Manager Communication
Intent: Explain a delay.
Expression: 非常抱歉给您带来困扰，这件事情没有达到我的预期，我深感自责，也十分感谢您的包容与理解。
Why it fails: It over-centers emotion, lacks useful information, and sounds performative.

### Example 3

Scene: Networking
Intent: Reach out after meeting someone briefly.
Expression: 您的经历让我深受震撼，我一直非常仰慕您，希望未来能有幸在您的指导下不断成长。
Why it fails: It is exaggerated, imbalanced, and creates distance rather than sincere connection.
